FAQ

Is this financial advice?

No. BasicPulse is an analytics tool. The Radar Score ranks how unusual a market looks right now; it does not predict returns. Crypto is volatile and you can lose everything.

Where does the data come from?

Public exchange APIs (Crypto.com, Binance, Bybit). No account or key is needed to use the free dashboard.

What is "basis"?

The gap between a perpetual future's price and spot. Persistent positive basis means longs are paying to hold (crowded-long); negative means shorts are paying. It's a widely-watched market-structure indicator.

Do you offer an API?

Yes — a JSON API with free and Pro keys, per-plan rate limits, premium signals and CSV export.

Can I self-host?

Yes. It's a small FastAPI app with a Dockerfile; the whole thing runs offline against a bundled snapshot if needed.
