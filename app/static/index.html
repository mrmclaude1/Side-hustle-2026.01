Start here — the 60-second tour

  1. The big table is the product. Every market, ranked by Radar Score (0–100) — how unusual it's acting right now, blending momentum, perp/spot basis, volatility and liquidity. The top 10 rows are where something is happening — no more scrolling 900 tickers.
  2. Click any row to expand its mini-charts: Score, Price and Basis history for that asset.
  3. Sort, search, filter. Click any column header to sort (try Basis — the most crowded trades). Press / to search. Tick Perps only for futures markets.
  4. History builds from snapshots. Each snapshot records the whole market; the Δ column and charts compare across them. A · dot means "no change since the last snapshot" — real markets move slowly, so give it time. (In production a schedule records one every 15 minutes; locally, visit /api/snapshot.)
  5. Alerts watch the market for you. Add a rule like Radar score ≥ 80 — every snapshot checks it, and triggers can push to Discord or Slack.
  6. Cross-Exchange shows the same coin priced on Binance, Bybit and Crypto.com — the arbitrage edge column flags crossed books (a chance to buy cheaper on one venue than you can sell on another).
